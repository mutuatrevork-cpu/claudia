Three ideas to remember

The profession in plain language

01

Start with the need

Educational Technologists identify learning and performance needs before choosing media, technology, or instructional strategies.

02

Design for people

The work combines instructional design, multimedia, accessibility, learning theory, and evaluation to create useful learning experiences.

03

Work across sectors

The same design skills can support education, business, healthcare, government, nonprofit, and military learning environments.

Where the field applies

Different sectors, different learning needs

EducationCourse design, digital curriculum, faculty or teacher support.
BusinessOnboarding, workforce development, compliance, and upskilling.
HealthcareClinical learning, simulation, patient education, and continuing education.
GovernmentWorkforce training, public-service learning, and community education.
MilitaryReadiness, technical instruction, systems design, and performance-based training.
